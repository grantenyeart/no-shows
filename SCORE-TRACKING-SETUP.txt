================================================================
TRACKING THE QUIZ SCORES
Three minutes of setup, then it runs itself.
================================================================

WHAT YOU GET
A Google Sheet that fills in automatically every time a setter
submits. One row each: name, score, pass or fail, which questions
they missed, and the timestamp.


----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1  Make the sheet
----------------------------------------------------------------
New Google Sheet. Name it "No-Show Quiz Scores".
Put these in row 1, A through F:

   Timestamp | Name | Score | Out Of | Passed | Missed


----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2  Add the script
----------------------------------------------------------------
In that sheet: Extensions -> Apps Script.
Delete whatever is in there, paste this, and save.

function doPost(e) {
  var sheet = SpreadsheetApp.getActiveSpreadsheet().getSheets()[0];
  var d = JSON.parse(e.postData.contents);
  sheet.appendRow([
    d.when || new Date(),
    d.name || '',
    d.score,
    d.total,
    d.passed ? 'PASS' : 'FAIL',
    d.missed || ''
  ]);
  return ContentService.createTextOutput('ok');
}


----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3  Deploy it
----------------------------------------------------------------
Deploy -> New deployment.
Type: Web app.
Execute as: Me.
Who has access: ANYONE.          <- this one matters
Deploy, approve the permissions, and copy the Web app URL.

It looks like:
https://script.google.com/macros/s/AKfy....../exec


----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4  Give me the URL
----------------------------------------------------------------
Send it to me and I will drop it into quiz.html and push.
Line 2 of the script block:

   const LOG_URL = "";

That is the only change needed. Until it is filled in, the quiz
still grades itself and shows the score, it just tells them to
screenshot it instead of logging automatically.


================================================================
IF YOU WOULD RATHER NOT DEPLOY ANYTHING
================================================================
Google Form in quiz mode does the same job with no code:

  New Google Form -> Settings -> make it a Quiz
  Paste the 15 questions, mark the right answer, 1 point each
  Responses tab -> link to a Sheet

Slower to build, but it auto-grades, emails them their score, and
you own it outright. The question bank is in quiz.html if you want
to copy them across, and I can write them out flat if you prefer.


================================================================
WHAT TO DO WITH THE SCORES
================================================================
Pass mark is 12 of 15. Below that is not a knowledge gap, it is a
"has not watched the training" gap, so send them back to it rather
than explaining it on a call.

The question they miss tells you what to coach:

   Q1, Q2 missed    They still think no-shows are a scheduling
                    problem. Nothing else will stick until that
                    changes.
   Q3, Q4, Q5       They are booking anyone who will say yes.
                    Pull their last 10 bookings and ask what the
                    pain was on each.
   Q6, Q7, Q8       They are booking a sales call. This one
                    costs you the most show rate.
   Q9, Q10          Not asking the commit question at all.
   Q11, Q12         Hanging up and hoping.
   Q13, Q14         Running half the sequence.
   Q15              Giving up at three attempts.
